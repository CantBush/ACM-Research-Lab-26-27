# Deliverable 2 — Core techniques

**Week 5.** Tuesday, Oct 27 and Thursday, Oct 29 are the same meeting. Attend one. The agenda is a short brief on forward and backward chaining and on RAG, a check-in, and help starting the experiment. The replication paper is chosen by a blind vote on a form, open through both meetings. The result is sent to the whole lab after both meetings.

## Tasks

- [ ] Run a small experiment that is **either** RAG **or** chaining (forward or backward). You pick the size. You can write it from scratch or use a library; if you use a library, explain what it does for you.
- [ ] Read Marco Tulio Ribeiro, Sameer Singh, and Carlos Guestrin, [*"Why Should I Trust You?": Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04938) (KDD 2016), and write a one-paragraph summary of it.

## Done when

- The experiment runs and you can show what it did.
- The paragraph is in your own words and names *"Why Should I Trust You?"*.

## Turn in

Turn in a one-page writeup and a link to your repo (`week-5/` folder), as described in [Turning in work](turn-in.md). The paragraph can go in the writeup.
