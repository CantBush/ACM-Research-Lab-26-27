# Deliverable 3 — Start the replication

**Week 6.** Tuesday: form small teams, break the chosen paper into pieces, and assign who implements which parts. Thursday: progress check and debugging help.

## Tasks

- [ ] Join a team of 2 or 3 and create a shared team repo.
- [ ] As a team, implement the paper’s main algorithm. Each member owns at least one piece.

## Done when

- Your team's implementation of the main algorithm runs, or each piece runs on its own if they are not connected yet.
- Each member can point to the piece they wrote.

## Turn in

Turn in a one-page writeup and a link to your team repo (`week-6/` folder), as described in [Turning in work](turn-in.md). Each member turns in their own writeup, covering the piece they own.
