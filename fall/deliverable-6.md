# Deliverable 6 — Write the paper

**Week 9.** The only meeting is Tuesday, Nov 24. Thursday, Nov 26 is a campus holiday, so members who usually come Thursday come Tuesday that week. The meeting is help on the paper structure, the assigned sections, and the peer review. Teams exchange reviews in writing by the Saturday deadline.

## Tasks

- [ ] As a team, write a full draft of your paper on the replication and extension. Target 4 pages, not counting references, in a NeurIPS or ICLR workshop template.
- [ ] Write your assigned sections.
- [ ] Peer-review one other team's draft. As a team, write down its strengths, its weaknesses, and 3 concrete fixes, and send them to that team.

## Done when

- A full draft exists, with every section written.
- Your team has sent its review to the other team.

## Turn in

In the Notion row, link the paper draft as the doc and the team repo (`week-9/` folder) as the repo; see [Turning in work](turn-in.md).
